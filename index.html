📚 GoHighLevel Glossary
An expanded reference for key CRM, automation, sales and operations terms.
GoHighLevel CRM Terms
Contact
A person or business record stored in GoHighLevel used for messaging, tracking, and sales activity.
Lead
A contact that has shown interest and is being tracked for follow-up by sales or marketing workflows.
Opportunity
A sales deal record that tracks potential revenue and client progress through pipeline stages.
Pipeline
A visual board of opportunity stages used to manage and forecast sales progress.
Pipeline Stage
A step inside a pipeline that shows where a deal currently stands, such as Qualified or Won.
Conversation
A message thread that brings together SMS, email, and social inbox messages for a contact.
Tag
A label applied to contacts or opportunities to organize, filter, and trigger automations.
Smart List
A saved contact view that automatically updates when records match the chosen filters and rules.
Custom Field
A user-created data field for storing unique client, lead, or opportunity details.
Custom Value
A reusable variable used in templates, funnels, and workflows to personalize automation content.
Automation Terms
Workflow
A sequence of actions that runs automatically when a trigger condition is met.
Trigger
An event that starts a workflow, such as a form submission, tag added, or appointment booked.
Action
A task performed inside a workflow, like sending a message, updating a field, or creating a task.
Wait Step
A delay inside a workflow that pauses the automation until a set time or event happens.
If/Else Branch
A workflow split that routes contacts down different paths based on specific conditions.
Campaign
A group of marketing messages and automation steps sent to contacts over time.
Nurture Sequence
A planned series of messages designed to build trust and move leads toward a sale.
Missed Call Text Back
An automated SMS reply sent when a lead misses a phone call to keep the conversation active.
Sales & Client Operations Terms
Discovery Call
The first call to learn about a prospect's needs and qualify them for your service.
Proposal
A formal offer that outlines the project scope, pricing, and deliverables for a client.
Quote
A documented price estimate for services or products shared with a prospect.
Work Order
A task or job assignment used to organize delivery details and track completion.
Client Onboarding
The process of bringing a new client into your systems and setting expectations for delivery.
Vendor Onboarding
The process of adding a new partner or supplier to your operational workflows.
Property Manager Onboarding
A tailored onboarding process for clients who manage rentals, facilities, or real estate portfolios.
Job Completion
The final stage when a service or project is finished and the client is ready for follow-up.
Operations Architect Terms
Digital Friction
Any process or tool that slows down customer interactions or internal workflows.
Inbox Blizzard
A flooded inbox of messages and tasks that makes it hard to prioritize real work.
Silent Architecture
An automation foundation that runs reliably behind the scenes so users don't have to manage every detail.
Load-Bearing System
A core process or tool that supports the entire business operation.
Digital Debt
The cost of outdated or poorly documented systems that slow down future growth.
Human-in-the-Loop
A process where automation hands off decisions to a person for review or escalation.
Single Source of Truth
The central system where the most accurate and up-to-date data lives for the entire team.
Lead-to-Revenue Workflow
The connected process from lead capture through nurturing to final payment and reporting.

📤 How Certification Submission Works

Once payment is processed successfully, students are redirected to the submission environment where they upload output and receive confirmation.

Payment Completed
The student completes payment and is routed to the certification submission form.
Redirect to Submission Form
Students are sent to a GoHighLevel form or funnel built for submission intake.
Student Uploads Evidence
Students submit folder links, screenshots, URLs, and documentation for review.
Google Sheet Records Submission
A connected Google Sheet logs each submission with tracking fields.
Confirmation Message
A thank-you confirmation appears after submission, reassuring students that review is next.
AI Review
An AI-assisted evaluation checks evidence completeness and workflow structure.
Human Verification
A human reviewer verifies the AI findings and confirms the final outcome.
Pass / Revision Needed
The submission is marked as Passed or Revision Needed based on evidence quality.
Certificate Issued
A certificate is issued once the student meets the criteria.
This is a competency-based certification. You are not certified for watching videos. You are certified for building, documenting, and implementing working GoHighLevel systems.
