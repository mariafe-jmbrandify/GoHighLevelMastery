# Certification Review Scorecard

Use this scorecard to review portfolio submissions for the GHL Mastery Practitioner and Operations Architect certifications.

## Review Criteria

| Category | Points | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Funnel or workflow clarity | 20 | Does the submitted build solve a clear operational problem? |
| CRM/pipeline structure | 20 | Are fields, tags, stages, and data flow organized? |
| Automation logic | 25 | Are triggers, actions, conditions, and notifications reliable? |
| Documentation quality | 15 | Can another operator understand and maintain the setup? |
| Business impact | 20 | Does the system reduce manual work or improve follow-up? |

## Outcome

- 85-100: Pass
- 70-84: Revision requested
- Below 70: Resubmit after additional build work
